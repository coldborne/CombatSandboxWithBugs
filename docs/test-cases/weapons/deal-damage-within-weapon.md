## test-case-002 - Deal damage within weapon

### Preconditions
- Player has at least one ammo in magazine
- Player is not reloading
- Player is not inside vehicle
- Dummy on the Scene

### Steps
1. Approach the target within the weapon's firing range
2. Aim at target dummy
3. Press left mouse button

### Expected Result
Raycast hits target dummy, damage is applied. There is a shot effect on the target.
