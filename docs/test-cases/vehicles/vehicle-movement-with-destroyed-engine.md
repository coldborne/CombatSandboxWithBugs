## test-case-007 - Vehicle movement with destroyed engine

### Preconditions
- Player is alive
- Player is not inside vehicle
- Vehicle exists on the map
- Engine hitbox exists in vehicle
- Engine module health is 0 (destroyed)

### Steps
1. Move within the radius of interaction with vehicle
2. Press `E` to enter vehicle
3. Press `W`

### Expected Result
Vehicle do not moves forward

3. Press `S`

### Expected Result
Vehicle do not moves backward.
