# BUG-002

## Summary
When exiting a vehicle player enters the textures

## Severity
Major

## Priority
High

## Environment
- Scene: `MainScene`
- Platform: Windows

## Preconditions
- MainScene is on
- Player is alive
- Vehicle exists on the map
- Player is inside vehicle
- Vehicle is near `ExitObstacle`
- Obstacle is located near vehicle right side

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Move or position vehicle close to `ExitObstacle` (Exit from the vehicle must be on the side of the obstacle)
2. Press `E` to exit vehicle
3. Observe player position after exit

## Expected Result
Player should exit to a valid free position near vehicle.

## Actual Result
Player is placed inside obstacle geometry.
