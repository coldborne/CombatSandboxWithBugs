# BUG-004

## Summary
When entering a vehicle, Player does not "climb" into the vehicle, but remains outside it. Control of the vehicle is retained

## Severity
Major

## Priority
Normal

## Environment
- Scene: `MainScene`
- Platform: Windows
- Vehicle: Any

## Preconditions
- MainScene is on
- Player is alive
- Player is not in vehicle
- Vehicle exists on the map
- Driver seat is empty

## Steps
1. Move within the radius of interaction with vehicle
2. Press `E` to enter vehicle
3. Observe player position after enter

## Expected Result
Player "climb" into the vehicle. Player's texture "disappears" because it is located inside the vehicle. Player can control the vehicle.

## Actual Result
Player can still control the vehicle, but its texture is frozen in place and still outside the vehicle.
