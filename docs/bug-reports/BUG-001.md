# BUG-001

## Summary
Weapon reloading is reset and does not fully complete when entering a vehicle.

## Severity
Major

## Priority
Normal

## Environment
- Engine: Unity
- Scene: `MainScene`
- Platform: Any

## Preconditions
- MainScene is on
- Player is alive
- Player has weapon
- Magazine is not full
- Ammo reserve is greater than 0
- Vehicle exists on the map
- Vehicle driver seat is empty

## Steps
1. Move player within the radius of interaction with vehicle
2. Press `R` to start reload
5. Until reloading is complete press E near vehicle
7. Exit vehicle
8. Pay attention to ammo in player inventory and magazine.

## Expected Result
Reload should be safely completed before player enters vehicle. Ammo in the magazine should reload and be removed from the inventory.

## Actual Result
Player can enter vehicle while reload is active.
Entering a vehicle interrupts the reload, preventing it from completing correctly. Ammo in the magazine remains unchanged
