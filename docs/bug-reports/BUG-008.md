# BUG-008

## Summary
Vehicle continues to move even after the engine is destroyed

## Severity
Blocker

## Priority
High

## Environment
- Scene: `MainScene`
- Platform: Windows

## Preconditions
- MainScene is on
- Player is alive
- Player is not in vehicle
- Vehicle exists on the map
- Driver seat is empty
- Vehicle`s engine is not destroyed

## Steps
1. Destroy vehicle`s engine
2. Move within the radius of interaction with vehicle
3. Press E to enter vehicle
3. Try to move the vehicle

## Expected Result
Vehicle do not continues to move

## Actual Result
Vehicle continues to move even after the engine is destroyed
